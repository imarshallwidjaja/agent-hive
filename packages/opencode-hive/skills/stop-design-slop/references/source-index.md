## Notes on interpretation

- Sources 4–10 support general claims about clutter, visual complexity, prototypicality, first impressions, and multidimensional aesthetics. They do **not** directly say "never use cards" or "gradients are bad".
- Sources 11–24 support the skill's translation of the rules into hierarchy/grouping/layout/component-system practice.
- Sources 25–30 are constraints: distinctiveness must not compromise accessibility.
- Sources 31–35 support the motivating concern that AI-assisted creative/design outputs can fixate or converge. This is an emerging research area, so the skill treats "anti-slop" tests as heuristics rather than scientific laws.
