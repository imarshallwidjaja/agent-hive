## Directional sentence and heading depth (2026-11-13)

An oc-arkive policy change, not from the imported source, gives this skill the depth behind the new `unslop` baseline rules. Principle 3 now asks for literal non-human verbs and new information at the end of the sentence; Principle 5 defines headings by their content, with exemptions for required formats, numbered plan tasks, and procedure steps. Cadence names the unasked trailing tail and significance phrases. The em dash substitute now pairs a field label with its value, and a classifying label opener becomes its statement. Boundaries applies the consequence test to attached contrasts and states required limits as their own facts; an evidence classification written as a contrast becomes what the evidence is and what was not done or observed. The references gain constructed examples and source notes (Williams; Gopen and Swan; the plain-language headings guide; Anthropic's "mannered prose" guidance; two consulted catalogs, with no text copied). Retained source wording: the existing sentences of the seven principles, the em dash rule's anti-semicolon clause, and every pinned sentence. The skill's own figurative wording ("genuinely easy", "would not land") and two rhetorical tails were made literal.
