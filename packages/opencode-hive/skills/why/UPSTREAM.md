# Provenance

Adapted from Lauren Tan's pstack 0.15.5 `why` skill, epistemics, investigator/synthesizer prompts, and source playbooks. Source: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/fae2c6ed95821bd85f614a73e4842e13229fa5e5/pstack/skills/why . Pinned revision `fae2c6ed95821bd85f614a73e4842e13229fa5e5`; imported 2026-10-01. MIT notice in `LICENSE`.

Preserved: evidence over plausible narrative, five historical confidence tiers, contradictions, counterfactuals, search records, and "The user's guess is a prompt for investigation, not a conclusion to validate."

Host adaptations: explicit historical trigger; no Cursor paths, model registry, writable investigators, fixed seven-source roster, or assumed Git/forge/MCP availability. Source guides are consolidated into capability-based selection. The primary synthesizes; Scouts collect evidence. Inferred is no longer described as well-supported, which overlapped the Supported tier. Direct testimony establishes recorded rationale, not its truth today. Empty, unavailable, and unsearched are distinct. Findings are planning inputs, not operator constraints or automatic durable-context writes. Output scales to the question rather than requiring every heading.
