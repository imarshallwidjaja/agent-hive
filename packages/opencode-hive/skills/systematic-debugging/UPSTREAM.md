## Deliberate adaptations

These are intentional changes to what the skill asks of an agent, not wording cleanup. The purpose of the skill, a fix that rests on causal evidence, is unchanged.

- **Four phases became a causal-evidence bar and selectable techniques.** Upstream asks for Phases 1 to 4 in order and, for a multi-component system, diagnostic instrumentation at every component boundary before any analysis. The skill now states when investigation may stop (the failure observed, the first wrong behavior located, the mechanism explained and competing explanations excluded or named as open) and lets the agent choose techniques by the question the evidence leaves open. The investigation steps of Phases 1 to 3 (read errors, reproduce, check recent changes, observe component boundaries, compare with working code, test one hypothesis) are reworded in `references/diagnostic-techniques.md`, each with the question it answers and when it ends. The component-boundary example is kept there for cases where the failing boundary is unknown. The reason is that the amount of investigation needed differs between settled evidence (a stack trace on one changed line, reproduced) and competing causes that need a discriminating observation, and a fixed traversal spends effort in the first case without ensuring the discriminating observation in the second. The Iron Law no longer refers to Phase 1, and the Quick Reference table of phases is replaced by a table of techniques by open question.
- **Red Flags, redirections and rationalizations.** "Return to Phase 1" now reads as a return to the causal-evidence bar, and the rationalization rows about simple issues and emergencies refer to the evidence bar instead of "process". The Red Flags items "Skip the test, I'll manually verify" and "One more fix attempt (when already tried 2+)", the closing line about three failed fixes, and the "test after" and repeated-attempt rationalization rows were already adapted for the Hive testing strategy and shared-premise reassessment.
- **Attempt limits.** Upstream's "three failed fixes means question the architecture" became a shared-premise reassessment. The skill now says that reassessing a premise changes the question the next attempt investigates and never resets or extends a role's retained attempt budget (a Forager keeps three iterations and three failed approaches), and that diagnosis-only work stays report-only. Role limits and recovery remain defined by the role, not by this skill.
- **Earlier adaptations that remain.** Phase 1 step 5, "Trace Data Flow", is "Downstream Symptoms and Hidden Writes". The mandatory failing test is a choice of testing and verification strategy with optional bounded preparatory refactoring. "No bundled refactoring" is replaced by that preparation step. The "No Root Cause" section is retitled "When Investigation Finds No Local Root Cause" and drops its "95%" claim. The "Violating the letter..." line and the Real-World Impact section are removed. Skill references use Hive's names and host-specific skill calls.
- **Supporting techniques.** Upstream's sibling documents `root-cause-tracing.md`, `defense-in-depth.md` and `condition-based-waiting.md` are not part of this skill. Their ideas appear as short techniques in the reference (waiting on a condition, validating at actual boundaries); the retained-state protections stay in `SKILL.md`.
