The source-tracing method, concrete explanation standard, and gap handling retain the source wording where possible. Host adaptations remove Cursor model choices, fixed fan-out, and the separate explainer dispatch. Scouts retrieve and the primary synthesizes. Prospective placement goes to the existing advisory path, current failures to debugging, and historical intent to `why`. Teaching guidance is conditional; no mandatory diagrams, image generation, history sweep, or independent prose policy is imported.
