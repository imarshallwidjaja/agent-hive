# Selective pstack material

Real-artifact and measurement guidance draws on Lauren Tan's `principle-prove-it-works`, `principle-make-operations-idempotent`, and `poteto-mode/playbooks/perf-issue.md` from pstack 0.15.5, `cursor/plugins` revision `fae2c6ed95821bd85f614a73e4842e13229fa5e5`. Source: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/fae2c6ed95821bd85f614a73e4842e13229fa5e5/pstack/skills . `LICENSE.pstack` covers those passages. Hive's evidence applicability, attribution, verdicts, and permission boundaries remain authoritative. The playbook workflow is not imported.

The measured-number and eval-result guidance in `SKILL.md` adapts Lauren Tan's `principle-explain-the-number` from pstack 0.15.25, `cursor/plugins` revision `09d06eb4b97a1020ab31e84030e38baf1099c7dc`. Source: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/09d06eb4b97a1020ab31e84030e38baf1099c7dc/pstack/skills/principle-explain-the-number . `LICENSE.pstack` covers the passage and is identical at both revisions; the passages above stay at 0.15.5. Deliberate adaptations: the guidance applies only when a measured number or eval result is evidence for a claim; a descriptive delta may be reported with its mechanism unknown, and a causal claim needs an observed mechanism; run count and spread are required only when noise could change the conclusion. The upstream `benchmark-checklist` skill is not imported, and no profiling or benchmark stage becomes mandatory.
